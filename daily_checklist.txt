 RULE OF THUMB (from tests so far)
  - Trade WITH the trend. Against the trend lost money in every test.
  - Avoid zones where big players sit (high volume, price not moving).
  - Enter when the wall breaks / after a pullback - not after a long move.
  - Most single-candle guesses = 50% (coin toss). Only situations at levels matter.
  - Every idea must beat option costs (~7 NIFTY pts per trade) to be worth it.
